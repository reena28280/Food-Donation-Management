About NourishLoop

Good food is too valuable to go to waste.

We connect surplus food with the people and community organizations who can use it, through a local network built around care, safety, and clear handoffs.

See how it works ↗
Share more.
Waste less.
01 THE CHALLENGE

A disconnect between surplus and need.

Restaurants, campuses, events, and households can have safe, edible food left over. At the same time, community kitchens and local organizations work to provide meals. Without a simple way to coordinate timing, quantity, and collection, good food can be wasted instead of shared.

NourishLoop focuses on that missing connection: making offers visible, requests manageable, and pickups coordinated among the people involved.

Our purpose

A practical loop with people at its center.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Make sharing workable.

Provide one place to list surplus food, find suitable offers, reserve servings, and coordinate collection with clear responsibilities.

03 / MISSION

Move good food to good use.

Help donors, receivers, and volunteers work together so available food can reach local communities safely and on time.

04 / VISION

A more thoughtful food system.

Imagine communities where sharing surplus is a normal, trusted part of how food moves, and less edible food is needlessly discarded.

How It Works

Three roles.
One good loop.

Surplus food moves from an offer to a confirmed handoff, with each person knowing what to do next.

Offer→Discover→Request→Collect
01FOR DONORS

Offer food you can share.

  1. Create a donor account and complete your organization and contact details.
  2. Once your profile is approved, post the servings, food type, allergens, storage, and pickup deadline.
  3. Review requests from receivers and accept or decline each one.
  4. Keep the pickup details private until the right people are involved, then confirm the completed handoff.
Donate food ↗
02FOR RECEIVERS

Find the food you need.

  1. Create a receiver account for your organization or community kitchen.
  2. After profile approval, search available food by city, keyword, and food type.
  3. Request the number of servings you can use. Your request reserves that quantity.
  4. When a donor accepts, coordinate collection and confirm the handoff after delivery.
Find food ↗
03FOR VOLUNTEERS

Help complete the journey.

  1. Create a volunteer account and complete your profile for approval.
  2. Open the delivery board to see accepted requests waiting for a volunteer.
  3. Claim a delivery to receive the exact pickup details.
  4. Update the progress from assigned to picked up to delivered.
Join as a volunteer ↗

Account approval: Anyone can browse public listings. Donors, receivers, and volunteers need an approved profile before posting food, requesting servings, or claiming deliveries.

Clear from start to finish

Reservations protect the servings.

Requests count against available portions right away, so food is not promised twice. A delivery is complete after the volunteer marks it delivered and both the donor and receiver confirm the handoff.

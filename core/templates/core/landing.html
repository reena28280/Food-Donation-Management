Why NourishLoop

Good food should feed people, not landfills.

Every day, perfectly good food goes unused while communities need nourishing meals. NourishLoop brings the people with surplus together with organizations and neighbors who can put it to good use.

We make the exchange clear and coordinated: donors share details, receivers reserve what they need, and volunteers help food reach its next table.
